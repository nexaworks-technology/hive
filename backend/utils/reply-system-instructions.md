# Reply System Instructions - Professional Email Response Framework

Based on SutraHR's proven outreach model, these instructions guide the auto-reply system to generate professional, value-driven responses to prospects.

## Email Response Structure

### 1. Greeting & Tone
- **Warm opening**: "Hello [Name]," or "Hello Sir/Ma'am,"
- **Tone**: Professional, consultative, friendly
- **Personal touch**: Reference their company or context from the initial outreach

Example:
```
Hello [Name],

Hope you are doing great. Before we jump on a call, I wanted to share a brief overview of how our [SERVICE] works.
```

---

## 2. Service Overview Section

### Primary Service Model (Similar to DRM - Dedicated Resource Model)
**Structure for your response:**
- Introduce your dedicated support model
- Explain the key benefits
- Mention unique sourcing/approach techniques

**Template:**
```
In our [YOUR_SERVICE_NAME], we provide you with [DEDICATED_RESOURCE] who will manage your end-to-end [SOLUTION_TYPE]. 
Over the years, we've built [YOUR_UNIQUE_ADVANTAGE], and with our [UNIQUE_TECHNIQUE], we are confident of [RESULTS_PROMISE].
```

**Example for Hive (Recruitment):**
```
In our Dedicated Outreach Model (DOM), we provide you with a dedicated manager who will handle your campaign end-to-end. 
Over the years, we've built a strong database of verified leads, and with our Hive Ninja sourcing techniques, 
we are confident of closing multiple qualified meetings within a week.
```

---

## 3. How It Works Section

**Format:** Keep it simple - 3-4 bullet points

**Required Elements:**
- Timeline/Pricing
- Resource Assignment
- Your Unique Process
- Supporting Tools/Databases

**Example Structure:**
```
How does this work?
• You pay [PRICING/COMMITMENT]
• We assign a [DEDICATED_RESOURCE] (minimum [QUALIFICATION])
• [RESOURCE] understands your needs and uses our [TECHNIQUE] to find the best [OUTCOMES]
• Access to [YOUR_TOOLS/PLATFORMS]
```

---

## 4. Why Choose Us Section

**3-5 Key Benefits:**
- Speed/Efficiency
- Cost Advantage  
- No Hidden Charges
- Flexibility
- Dedicated Support

**Example:**
```
Why choose our service?
• Fastest results to save you time and cost
• No multi-year contracts — Pay only for [WHAT_YOU_GET]
• No additional billing on [OUTCOMES] and no hidden charges
• Dedicated account management
• Real-time support and updates
```

---

## 5. Client Expectations Section

**Set clear expectations (3-4 points):**
- Response time commitment (usually 24 hours)
- Availability requirements
- Feedback process
- Communication preference

**Example:**
```
What do we expect from you?
• All feedback within 24 hours
• Availability for discussions/meetings on time
• Clear communication of your needs and priorities
• Openness to our recommendations based on market data
```

---

## 6. Pricing/Investment Section

**Be transparent and specific:**
- Clearly state investment amount
- Explain what's included
- Mention ROI or value delivered
- Note any conditional adjustments

**Example Structures:**
```
For these services, we charge [AMOUNT] per [TIMEFRAME] before commencement.

Alternative model: [MODEL_NAME]
- Base investment: [AMOUNT]
- Performance-based adjustment: [PERCENTAGE] of [METRIC]
- Net investment after adjustment: [CALCULATION]
```

---

## 7. Alternative Options Section

**Always offer flexibility:**
- Present 2-3 model options
- Different price points
- Different commitment levels
- Different outcome metrics

**Example:**
```
Alternative model: [OPTION_NAME]
This allows us to [KEY_BENEFIT]

How it works:
• [STEP_1]
• [STEP_2]
• [STEP_3]

Rate: [PRICING_MODEL]
```

---

## 8. Social Proof & Authority Section

**Build credibility with:**
- Case studies link
- Client testimonials  
- Industry recognition
- Success metrics
- Downloadable resources (brochure, guide)

**Example:**
```
You can review our clients' case studies and testimonials here: [WEBSITE]/case-study

Here's the brochure for you to explore the companies we have worked with and the results we've delivered:
Click here to download our [SERVICE] Brochure (PDF)
```

---

## 9. Client References Section

**Include 3-5 verifiable references:**

**Format:**
```
References (as discussed)
[Name] – [Company] – [Phone]
[Name] – [Company] – [Phone]
[Name] – [Company] – [Phone]

These references reflect the quality of our work and our commitment to excellence.
```

**Why this works:**
- Provides social proof
- Shows confidence in service quality
- Gives prospect someone to call
- Builds trust through real examples

Thanks and Regards,

## Reply System Decision Tree

### When to use this framework:

1. **Prospect shows interest** → Send value-first response with overview
2. **Prospect asks about pricing** → Include pricing section + alternatives
3. **Prospect asks "how it works"** → Focus on "How It Works" + Benefits
4. **Prospect is hesitant** → Lead with Social Proof + References + Case Studies
5. **Prospect wants to close** → Summarize next steps and call-to-action

---

## Key Principles for Your Replies

✅ **DO:**
- Be specific about your offering (not vague)
- Provide multiple options/models
- Include concrete numbers and timelines
- Show proof (references, case studies, testimonials)
- Make pricing transparent
- Set clear expectations upfront
- Include a specific next step
- Use their name when possible
- Reference their context/company

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use generic corporate language
- Hide pricing or terms
- Make unrealistic promises
- Overwhelm with too much info (keep focused)
- Forget the human touch
- Miss the clear call-to-action
- Ignore their specific objections

## Customization Variables for Your Business

Update these based on YOUR service (Recruitment, Outreach, etc):

- `[YOUR_SERVICE]` = Your main offering
- `[DEDICATED_RESOURCE]` = Your dedicated support person type
- `[UNIQUE_TECHNIQUE]` = Your proprietary method/tool
- `[RESULTS_PROMISE]` = Specific outcome you deliver
- `[PRICING]` = Your investment model
- `[CASE_STUDIES_LINK]` = Your proof/testimonial URL
- `[REFERENCES]` = Real clients willing to be references

## Final Note

This framework is designed to:
1. **Build trust** through transparency and proof
2. **Educate** prospects on your value
3. **Simplify decision** by offering clear options
4. **Reduce friction** with pricing upfront
5. **Enable action** with specific next steps

The Huma Madad email does all of this. Your reply system should follow this same pattern while being customized for your specific service/product.
